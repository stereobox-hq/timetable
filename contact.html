Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you fill an emergency shift? +

We aim to fill emergency shifts within 2–4 hours. Our on-call coordinators operate 24/7 and maintain a pool of available healthcare workers who can be deployed at short notice.

Are all your staff DBS checked? +

Yes — 100% of our workers hold a valid Enhanced DBS certificate. We check the DBS Update Service before every placement and can provide certificates on request.

What areas do you cover? +

We are based in Crewe, Cheshire and primarily serve care homes across Cheshire, Staffordshire, and Greater Manchester. Contact us to discuss your location.

How does invoicing work? +

We issue weekly invoices based on timesheets confirmed by your management team. Payment terms are 14 days from invoice by bank transfer. Contact us to set up a credit account.

Are there minimum booking hours? +

We have a minimum booking of 4 hours per shift. Most placements run for 8 or 12 hours, aligned with standard care home rota patterns for continuity of care.
