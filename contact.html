Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you fill an emergency shift? +

We aim to fill emergency shifts within 2–4 hours. Our on-call coordinators operate 24/7 and maintain a pool of available healthcare workers who can be deployed at short notice. For non-emergency planned cover, we ask for as much notice as possible.

Are all your staff DBS checked? +

Yes — 100% of our workers hold a valid Enhanced DBS certificate. We check the DBS Update Service before every placement to ensure there have been no changes since the certificate was issued. We can provide DBS certificates upon request.

What areas do you cover? +

We are based in Crewe, Cheshire and primarily serve care homes across Cheshire, Staffordshire, and Greater Manchester (North West England). We can sometimes arrange cover further afield — please contact us to discuss your location.

How does invoicing work? +

We issue weekly invoices based on timesheets confirmed by your management team. Payment terms are 14 days from invoice. We accept bank transfer. Contact us to set up a credit account for ongoing bookings.

Are there minimum booking hours? +

We have a minimum booking of 4 hours per shift. Most standard shift patterns are 8 or 12 hours, and we encourage bookings that align with your existing rota patterns for continuity of care.
