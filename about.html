What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Every member of the Bluey Healthcare team — from our office coordinators to our healthcare workers — is guided by three foundational values.

🔒

When we say we'll fill a shift, we mean it. We operate 24/7 precisely because care never stops — and neither do we. No excuses, no last-minute cancellations.

🏅

❤️

Our Standards

Every Worker is Thoroughly Vetted

We take our responsibilities seriously. Before any healthcare worker is placed with one of our partner care homes, they must pass our multi-stage screening process.

  • Enhanced DBS (Disclosure & Barring Service) check
  • Verification of professional registrations (NMC, etc.)
  • Two professional references — contacted directly
  • Right to work in the UK documentation
  • Mandatory training: Manual Handling, Fire Safety, Safeguarding, Infection Control
  • Competency assessment relevant to role
  • COVID-19 vaccination verification
  • Uniform policy compliance and professional conduct brief
