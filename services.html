Registered General Nurses (RGN)

Our RGNs are fully qualified, NMC pin-verified nurses with current clinical experience in care home and hospital settings. Each nurse is rigorously screened, reference-checked, and competency-assessed before placement.

In a care home setting, our RGNs cover:

  • Administration of prescribed medications (oral, topical, injections)
  • Complex wound care and dressing management
  • Clinical assessments and resident monitoring
  • Catheter care and PEG tube management
  • Palliative and end-of-life care support
  • Liaison with GPs, district nurses and families
  • Infection prevention and control oversight
  • Documentation and care plan management

£16.50/hr
QCF Level 2/3 · Care Experienced

Healthcare Assistants (HCA)

Our HCAs are empathetic, experienced care workers who provide high-quality personal care and daily living support. All hold relevant QCF qualifications and have a minimum of 12 months' care home experience.

Our HCAs support residents with:

  • Personal care — washing, dressing, grooming, continence
  • Meal preparation and nutritional monitoring
  • Mobility assistance and manual handling
  • Companionship and wellbeing activities
  • Assisting nurses with clinical tasks
  • Accurate record-keeping and handover notes
  • Pressure area care and repositioning
  • Dementia-sensitive, person-centred care

Team Leader / Med Tech
£18/hr
Senior Care · Leadership Qualified

Team Leaders & Medication Technicians

Our senior care staff are experienced leaders who can take charge of a shift, supervise junior care workers, and manage medication rounds with precision and accountability.

Team Leaders and Med Techs provide:

  • Shift leadership and care team supervision
  • Safe medication administration and MAR documentation
  • Medication Administration Record (MAR) reconciliation
  • Controlled drug management and safe storage oversight
  • Staff allocation and handover coordination
  • Incident reporting and escalation management
  • Supporting CQC compliance and care plan reviews
  • Acting as point of contact for families and professionals

Domestic Workers

A clean, well-maintained environment is essential to resident wellbeing and CQC compliance. Our domestic staff are experienced in care home environments and understand the specific hygiene, infection control, and safeguarding standards required.

Our Domestic Workers handle:

  • Full cleaning and sanitisation of all areas
  • Laundry collection, washing, drying and distribution
  • Kitchen and dining room preparation and clearing
  • Waste management and clinical waste disposal
  • COSHH-compliant use of cleaning products
  • Deep cleaning and infection control protocols
  • Catering support — meal preparation and serving
  • Maintaining CQC cleanliness standards
